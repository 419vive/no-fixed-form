The editorial approach

Move from a quiet surface to stored stress, a slipping fault, seismic waves, and the local experience of shaking. Each visual explains one part of the narration.

What I made

Script, original animated diagrams, edit, chapter typography, and sound leveling. The narration uses the ElevenLabs engine through Higgsfield, with a preset male voice.

Evidence and boundaries

This is a self-initiated motion-graphics explainer, not a commissioned client project. All diagrams are schematic and not to scale. Building motion and wave traces are illustrative, not measured data. No disaster footage is presented as evidence.

This sample demonstrates researched narration and explanatory editing. It does not demonstrate a full-length, archival-footage documentary workflow.
